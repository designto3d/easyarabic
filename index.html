    FAQ

    Questions? Answered.

    I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

    Absolutely. Every program starts from the basics. Many students begin with the free video course and then move to live classes.

    Which platform do classes use?

    All live classes run on Zoom or Google Meet — whichever you prefer. You only need an internet connection and a device with a microphone.

    What's the difference between 1-1 and combined classes?

    1-1 classes are private, flexible and fully tailored to you. Combined classes are small live groups with fixed weekly timings and a lower price.

    Are there classes for children?

    Yes — Quran with Tajweed and Easy Arabic 1-1 classes are available for children as well as adults.

    Do I need the book for the classes?

    The classes follow Easy Arabic Grammar, so having it helps you revise. You can read the first 10 pages free on this site.

    How does the free trial work?

    Send the booking form below. We'll contact you to agree a time, then you'll get a meeting link for your free trial class.
